# Codex review: gpt-6.1-sol

The skirt reads as a continuous bell, but the clay and three-quarter views expose a broad, relatively flat rear-side panel and an uneven ridge above the hem that make it feel more like a molded shell than hanging fabric. Its most urgent animation issue is the squat: the front lifts into a broad shelf while the side stretches into a long wedge, exposing the petticoat instead of gathering into rounded folds around the thighs. Reconstruct the lower-skirt surface with continuous longitudinal curvature before adjusting its shading normals, then refine the sector drape around the raised legs. Fidelity work should concentrate on the hem's fold rhythm and the amount of pink underskirt revealed, rather than pursuing the already high front and back silhouette IoU. These ratings assess the supplied Blender stills; they do not establish equivalent deformation or collision behavior after PMX export.

Scores: naturalness 5/10, animation 6/10, fidelity 6/10

## skirt

- **P1** [naturalness] *clay side, clay q34_back_right, q34_front_left* - Rear-side lower skirt, from approximately mid-thigh height to the trailing hem: A broad side panel descends almost as a straight triangular sheet and finishes in a projecting rear corner. The three-quarter view exposes a large planar area between the rounded front folds and the back, so the volume reads as a rigid cone panel rather than cloth supported by the petticoat.
  - Fix: In skirt.py, regularize the sampled radius field in _target() over the rear-side sectors before profiles() builds the shell. Replace the long straight radial descent with a gently curved longitudinal profile and two broad fold transitions; start by reducing the projecting lower corner by 1–2 cm and distributing that change across the lower 8–12 cm. Use a monotone cubic profile through the corrected samples instead of relying on slope clipping followed by linear interpolation, and preserve the existing inner clearance when rebuilding the petticoat.
- **P1** [naturalness] *clay front, clay q34_front_left, clay high* - Lower front and front-side folds, roughly 3–6 cm above the hem: Several folds swell into rounded knobs and then cross an approximately horizontal ridge before reaching the edge. This interruption is visible without cel shading, so changing the ramp alone will leave the skirt looking inflated or molded.
  - Fix: In _target(), smooth the longitudinal second differences of the radius residual specifically through the lower fold band, while retaining broad circumferential crest positions. Blend out isolated radial bulges by about 0.5–1 cm over a 4–6 cm vertical span, with a continuous first derivative at both ends. Make each principal fold run into its hem lobe without the common transverse shoulder; retain the shell's existing thin rounded edge.
- **P1** [animation] *squat front, squat q34_front_left* - Front hem over both thighs and the transition into the side skirt panels: The front is lifted as a wide shelf, while the side remains stretched into a long diagonal wedge. The large exposed pink underside makes the loss of hanging cloth especially apparent; the skirt clears the legs at the expense of believable fold compression.
  - Fix: Refine the skirt drape in rig.py so clearance lift stays local to the thigh-facing sectors and adjacent side sectors gather toward them at a lower height. In _drape_gather, trial reducing the free ring strain DRAPE_STRAIN from 0.30 to 0.20 and increasing the strain cost, while retaining the existing neighbor caps initially. If the three-segment chains still force a single long side plane, use four segments with a joint near the lower fold transition and recompute the drape tables and _skirt_weights interpolation. Keep outer, inner and rolled-hem vertices sharing the same cloth weights, and check both squat views after each adjustment so reduced stretch does not recreate a lifted bowl.
- **P2** [naturalness] *high, q34_front_left, islands high* - Front-right fold faces and scattered lower-front and hem shadow boundaries: The pale cloth has stepped, narrow shadow streaks and disconnected slivers rather than a few broad fold shadows. The high view contains 13 dress-region islands covering 315 pixels; that region also includes the bodice and sleeves, but several marked fragments are visibly on the skirt.
  - Fix: After correcting the surface, reduce the amount of fine sampled relief retained by skirt.py's _shade_normals: trial RELIEF_SHADE at 0.35–0.45 instead of 0.60 and increase RELIEF_SOFT_PX from 10 to approximately 14. Smooth the reference normals vertically as well as around the circumference, then normalize and blend them continuously through the hem-roll transition. Retain the broad fold turns; do not remove islands by changing the global ramp until the skirt's own normal field is clean.
- **P2** [animation] *walk front, walk q34_front_left* - Front hem immediately above the advancing thigh: The lifted lobe has a localized angular peak where the raised panel joins the neighboring hem. It is a small deformation defect here, but it interrupts the rounded fold contour during an ordinary stride.
  - Fix: In rig.py, inspect the two skirt chains bracketing this peak and spread their lift difference over the adjacent sectors using the existing gather stage. Smooth the vertical weight transition around the lower segment boundary over approximately 1–2 cm, preserving the maximum four influences and shared shell weights. Evaluate the walk pose at intermediate thigh pitches to check that the peak does not simply move to the next sector.
- **P2** [fidelity] *front, side, back in 01_ortho_sbs* - Entire hem circumference, especially the front-side arches and back edge: The model's edge is comparatively level and shallowly scalloped, with a narrow pink strip beneath it. The reference has larger alternating dropped lobes and raised arches that reveal substantially more petticoat. The missing rhythm changes the skirt's design even where the overall silhouette overlaps well.
  - Fix: Adjust target_hem() and the hem rows used in _dense() with a smooth periodic correction tied to the existing broad fold crests. Begin by raising the shallow arches by 1–2 cm, leaving the lowest lobes close to their current height, and ease the height correction through the lower 6–8 cm of profiles(). Keep SMOOTH_ROW_PX sufficient to round the arch tops. Rebuild the dependent petticoat from the revised surface and inspect the newly revealed inner hem in three-quarter views before increasing the correction.
- **P3** [fidelity] *front, back, q34_back_right* - Broad lower-skirt fold faces between the waist and hem: Large areas of the skirt remain nearly uniform ivory, while the reference separates its folds with more deliberate pale mint coloration. This contributes to the broad panels reading as empty areas; the supplied dress shade shares are also lower than the reference in front and back, although those measurements include other garments.
  - Fix: Once the geometry and normal field are stable, derive skirt.py's mint tint from the final sampled fold residual rather than only the approximate fold-table streak positions. Apply a broad, smoothly feathered tint on the recessed fold faces, increasing local gradient weight by approximately 0.05–0.10 as an initial trial. Keep the upper skirt light and evaluate the gradient in both toon tones so the added mint does not become a separate hard stripe.
