Give this phoneme a more rounded opening than tile 21.

4. **Tile 35: the tears read as attached droplets rather than fluid starting at the lid.** In 3/4 and side, a small skin-colored gap appears between the lower eyelid and the blue drop. **Fix:** extend a narrow tear neck to the lower-lid margin, place its root at the outer surface of the lid, and conform the descending portion to the cheek with a small surface offset. Check that the root remains attached as the eyelid moves.

5. **Tile 34: the sweat drop loses readability with head rotation.** It is visible in front but disappears in the supplied 3/4 and side views, consistent with placement on the far cheek. **Fix:** if this morph is intended to remain readable across these viewing angles, move the drop toward the temple or upper cheek silhouette and orient it along the local surface normal, or provide a separately controllable counterpart on the other side. A unilateral drop being occluded is physically reasonable; this is an expression-readability issue rather than proof of broken geometry.
