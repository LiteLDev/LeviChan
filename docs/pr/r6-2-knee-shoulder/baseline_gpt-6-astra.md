# Codex review: gpt-6-astra

Levi's exposed body generally holds its volume through the turntable, but the knees still read as bent tubes rather than articulated joints in the walk and squat poses. These are the first corrections to make: the body already has closely spaced knee loops, so adding more geometry alone is unlikely to resolve the deformation. The remaining supplied poses show no clearly visible body tearing or major interpenetration, although clothing conceals the elbows and much of the hip deformation. These scores assess the body and its visible interaction with the rig; the still images do not establish motion stability or PMX playback quality.
