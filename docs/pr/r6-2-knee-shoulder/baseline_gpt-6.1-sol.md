# Codex review: gpt-6.1-sol

The body reads coherently around the turntable, but knee deformation remains the clearest obstacle to animation readiness: the walk produces an angular joint, and the squat compresses the bend into a pinched fold. The existing body code already supplies dense knee loops and two deformation helpers, so the next pass should refine their weight transitions and preserve joint volume rather than add general mesh density. Raised arms show a smaller concern at the exposed underarms, where the shoulder-to-torso transition becomes thin and taut. Rest-pose improvements should concentrate on the knee-to-calf surface and hand volume; skin colour matching is a later polish task. This assessment is limited to the supplied renders and inspected build code; clothing obscures most elbow, hip and torso deformation, and the displayed poses do not establish finger-curl or wrist-twist quality.

Scores: naturalness 7/10, animation 6/10, fidelity 8/10

Refine the knee-specific weight split in levi/rig.py so ring-to-ring rotation changes continuously through the thigh, both knee helpers and the shin; inspect the resulting weights after four-influence pruning. Keep the current 7.5 cm overall band as the initial envelope, and tune the helper knots currently at -2.5/+2.5 cm against the actual knee loops rather than widening the whole band. - **P1** [animation] *squat q34_front_left, squat front* - both knees, compressed inner bend and front kneecap envelope: The deep bend bunches the knee into a tight fold with a pointed change of contour. The visible joint loses the rounded separation between thigh and calf, giving the squat a compressed, folded appearance.
  - Fix: Add a knee-specific volume correction to the existing helper scheme rather than increasing global smoothing. Use the current 33%/67% helpers to carry separate front-knee loop bands, and adjust their offsets so the outer bend remains convex at approximately 120 degrees. In body.py, start by reducing the narrow KNEE_CREASE indentation from 0.7 px to 0.3-0.4 px and broadening its sigma from 5 px to 7-8 px if that crease contributes to the compressed fold. Implement the retained-volume correction through PMX-exportable helper weights or additional deform helpers, and recheck the walk so fixing the squat does not create a swollen stride knee.
