# Codex review: gpt-6.1-sol

The petticoat is coherent in the calibrated front and back silhouettes, but the three-quarter views expose tall, flat pink panels and pointed hanging flaps that weaken its believability as gathered fabric. These are primarily rest-shape problems, visible in both cel and clay renders, rather than shading artifacts alone. The first correction should make the two tiers nest more closely and replace the abrupt transitions between tucked and exposed sections with rounded, continuous folds. The tested poses retain the layers without obvious tearing or collapse, although the walk and squat slightly flatten the exposed front band. After correcting the volume, refine the gather relief and pink shading; the petticoat already has few small shade islands, so broad shadow placement matters more than speck cleanup.

Scores: naturalness 5/10, animation 7/10, fidelity 6/10

## petticoat

- **P1** [naturalness] *q34_back_right, q34_front_left, clay q34_back_right* - Exposed side-quarter ruffles, especially the large pink flap at image-right in q34_back_right: Several exposed sections become tall, nearly planar hanging petals with pointed lower corners instead of soft gathered ruffles. They remain conspicuous in clay, and their silhouettes read as separate fins attached beneath the skirt.
  - Fix: In levi/parts/petticoat.py, revise _tier() so the transition from each tucked section to an exposed section changes the attachment ring and hem together, rather than primarily reshaping the hem beneath the existing top ring. Start by reducing tier0 EXPOSE_MAX from 42 to 28–30 px and EXPOSE from 28 to 20–22 px, then apply a comparable local exposure limit to tier1 where it produces the remaining fins. Blend the affected top-ring heights over at least one complete gather on either side and round the resulting lower contour; retain the current thin hem roll rather than thickening the flap.
- **P2** [naturalness] *side, q34_front_left, q34_back_right* - Space between the outer and inner tiers around the side and front quarters: The tiers separate into widely spaced nested bands: the inner tier sits substantially inward while the outer tier forms a broad overhang. Oblique views reveal the construction as two distinct shells rather than closely overlapping layers of one petticoat.
  - Fix: Replace tier1's uniform 0.70 hem-outline scale in TIERS with an outline derived from tier0 and an inward offset. Start with a 1.5–2.5 cm separation in the visible side-quarter sections, while preserving leg clearance at the center. Recalculate its attachment using _core_point(), and add a tier-to-tier clearance pass after _clamp_ring() so the gathered crests retain at least 0.4–0.6 cm of separation without opening a large radial gap.
- **P2** [naturalness] *front, q34_front_left, clay front* - Broad exposed pink faces below the raised skirt troughs: Most gathering is legible only along the scalloped edges; the exposed faces between those edges are broad and smooth. This makes the petticoat resemble stacked cutout bands rather than fabric gathered into its seams.
  - Fix: In _tier(), carry rounded gather relief farther up the exposed band. The current depth of 2.4–2.6 px is only about 0.4 cm; test 3.5–4.5 px locally on exposed sections, with a smooth fade beneath tucked sections instead of increasing depth everywhere. Add a support ring between PROFILE values 0.0 and 0.15 so the transition from the ungathered attachment ring does not concentrate into the first strip of faces. Keep SPF at least 8 and evaluate the actual relief in clay before changing FOLD_SHADE.
- **P2** [animation] *walk q34_front_left, squat q34_front_left* - Front tier faces and lower lip immediately above the advancing thigh or bent knees: The exposed front band becomes straighter and more taut in the walk and squat, reducing the soft overlap between tiers. The layers remain intact, but the result reads as a panel being carried over the legs rather than ruffles retaining some downward drape.
  - Fix: In levi/rig.py, derive corresponding tier weights from a shared undeformed garment coordinate, such as attachment azimuth and normalized distance down the supporting core, rather than independently from each gathered vertex's displaced position. Copy the edge weights onto its _hem_roll() return rings so the lip bends as one section. Preserve the existing skirt-chain system and 1.2 cm thigh-clearance target; if the front band remains taut, add a lower drape segment that can rotate downward relative to the lifted segment rather than adding direct thigh weights.
- **P2** [fidelity] *front, side, 01_ortho_sbs* - Visible pink ruffle band across the front and beneath the side skirt opening: The reference has a denser rhythm of small, overlapping ruffles, while the render presents longer uninterrupted pink strips with comparatively shallow scallops. The side petticoat region also occupies more area than the reference, consistent with the oversized exposed panels.
  - Fix: After correcting exposure and tier spacing, increase the gather counts in TIERS from 41/30 toward approximately 48/38 and stagger their phases so the two hems do not trace parallel waves. Raise scallop rise modestly from 4.5/4.0 px toward 5.0–5.5 px, retaining rounded notches. Adjust HEM_SIDE and the side-quarter exposure limits locally to reduce excess pink area instead of uniformly shrinking the entire petticoat.
- **P2** [fidelity] *front, side, high* - Lit faces and overlapping edges of the pink tiers: The front and side ruffles are too pale and insufficiently shaded relative to the sheet: shade coverage is 0.23 versus 0.39 at the front and 0.34 versus 0.44 at the side. The high view already reaches 0.63, so a uniform increase in darkness or shadow coverage would overcorrect other angles.
  - Fix: In levi/spec.py, test a slightly darker, less bright petticoat base, starting near #F7DAD3 instead of #FDE3DD. In petticoat._finish(), use a spatially varying normal blend: retain the soft blend on broad fabric faces but preserve more geometric normal around recessed gathers and turned hems. Keep CAST_TIERS disabled initially; recover narrow overlap shading locally rather than enabling full tier shadows, and recheck front, back and high views together.
- **P3** [naturalness] *q34_front_left, q34_back_right, 05_islands* - Small exposed hem tips and recessed side-quarter folds: A few isolated shade wedges remain on the oblique ruffle edges. Their total area is small, but they sharpen already pointed tips and make those locations look chipped.
  - Fix: After the shape changes, smooth the custom shading-normal blend along arc length over roughly one gather at the affected tips, while keeping the rolled hem's normals separate from the broad face treatment. Preserve sufficient geometric normal on the underside; do not reduce FOLD_SHADE globally to remove the remaining three front-quarter islands and single back-quarter island.
