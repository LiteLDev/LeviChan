**gpt-6.1-sol:** The model's main lookdev weakness is fragmented shading: pale hair and cloth acquire thin wedges, isolated flecks, and abrupt shade boundaries that make otherwise continuous surfaces look chipped or folded from card. The clay views confirm that some of these breaks originate in the geometry, so increasing shadow filtering or changing the global ramp alone will not resolve them. Prioritize coherent shading normals, smoother hair-tip colour transitions, and simpler cast-shadow silhouettes on the legs and skirt while preserving contact shade between garment layers. Then tune material-specific shade coverage and palette contrast; the strong silhouette IoU does not capture these interior shading problems. The pose images expose additional shading stress, but still images cannot establish temporal stability or verify the exported PMX appearance.

**gpt-6-astra:** The current shading exaggerates the model's segmented construction: angular hair shadow boundaries, chipped skirt folds, and broad scalloped shadows on the thighs prevent the character from reading as a softly formed volume across angles. The strongest problems are visible in both cel and clay renders, so changing the global threshold or light alone will not resolve them. The code already supports continuous palette gradients, custom hair normals, selective shadow casting, and variable outline widths; the next pass should use those controls locally before retuning palette values. Animation readiness remains provisional: the posed stills show conspicuous shadow fragmentation around bent cloth, but they cannot establish temporal stability or MMD shading parity. Prioritize coherent normals and shadow silhouettes, then reduce internal line clutter and restore the reference's restrained colour separation.
