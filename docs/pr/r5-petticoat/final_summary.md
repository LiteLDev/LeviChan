After those structural changes, reduce the excessive side exposure and refine the distribution of the ruffles to match the reference. These scores describe the petticoat and its interaction with the skirt; the supplied still poses cannot establish animated collision stability or PMX physics behavior.

**gpt-6-astra:** The petticoat reads as a coherent layered underskirt in the resting front view, but the oblique views reveal broad, shallow bands and locally abrupt hanging flaps rather than consistently gathered fabric. The most important shape correction is to give the tiers a more continuous three-dimensional drape, especially where the side tucks transition into the rear hem. In the squat, the pink layers spread into stacked shelves and expose much more of the underskirt, so rest-pose clearance alone is insufficient to establish animation readiness. Refine the tier profiles and their coordinated deformation before tuning the pink palette or matching the smaller reference scallops. The supplied stills support these deformation concerns but do not establish the behavior of exported PMX physics over time.
