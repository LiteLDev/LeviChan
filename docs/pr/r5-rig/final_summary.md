The broad, thin outer hair locks also undermine the secondary motion by reading as paddles when displaced. Prioritize the knee and overhead-arm corrections, then localize skirt lifting and verify the same poses after PMX export, since the Blender drape drivers and exported append-plus-physics behavior differ. These judgments use the supplied renders and inspected build code; PMX playback and deformation between the illustrated poses remain unverified.

**gpt-6-astra:** The rig preserves the character's overall shape in all six tests, with no clearly visible tearing or inverted joints, but the squat exposes a substantial knee-volume problem and the raised sleeves still compress into unnatural shapes. The strong orthographic silhouette scores do not establish deformation quality, and the Blender pose renders do not establish PMX parity because the skirt's nonlinear drape drivers are replaced by linear append contributions and physics on export.
