The supplied poses show limited torso deformation, so they do not establish that the lacing, facings and neckline ornament will remain attached through stronger bends or twists. Scores refer to the bodice and its attached details; the whole-character silhouette and dress-region metrics do not isolate this part.

**gpt-6-astra:** The bodice has a coherent torso volume, but its broad, nearly featureless front and narrow side shading make it read as a molded shell rather than the softly gathered fabric in the sheet. The most urgent bodice-related issue is the waist-to-ribbon transition in the squat: the hanging decoration bunches into the waist and loses its readable separation from the skirt. Next, refine the upper-bust gathers and the three-dimensional construction of the bow, then improve the lacing layout and neckline leaf cluster. These scores concern the bodice and its immediate interfaces; the supplied static poses support a deformation review, but do not establish motion continuity or PMX physics behavior.
