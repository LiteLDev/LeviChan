# Codex review: gpt-6-astra

Levi’s main lookdev problem is the shape and stability of the shadow regions: narrow hair slivers, sharply stamped cast shadows, and disconnected patches on posed clothing undermine otherwise readable volumes. The rear hair simultaneously looks too flat across its broad white surface and too noisy along individual strands; changing the global ramp threshold alone will not resolve both problems. Prioritize smoother, coherent shading normals and separate control of cast-shadow edges, then address the mint transitions and regional palette balance. Keep the two-tone style, but make its boundaries follow large forms rather than small surface irregularities. These scores reflect the supplied still images; temporal flicker and the final MMD shading remain unverified.

Scores: naturalness 6/10, animation 6/10, fidelity 7/10

## lookdev

- **P1** [naturalness] *back, q34_back_right, islands back* - White rear hair curtain, from crown to the start of the mint curls: The rear hair contains numerous short, isolated grey cuts instead of continuous shadow shapes. The island diagnostic finds 55 hair islands in back and 41 in q34_back_right, and the unmarked renders show the same chipped, scratchy surface. These marks interrupt the reading of smooth locks and are particularly conspicuous on the otherwise nearly flat white curtain.
  - Fix: Add a reusable shading-normal smoothing step before the toon material is evaluated, preserving strand boundaries and silhouette geometry. In hair_back.set_volume_normals(), test reducing the upper-lock own-normal contribution from VOL_KEEP = 0.55 to approximately 0.30–0.40, with a continuous transition toward the existing stronger curl normals. Compare shadows-on and --no-shadows renders to isolate cast-shadow slivers before tuning the ramp; do not try to remove all 55 islands by lowering the hair threshold.
- **P1** [naturalness] *side, q34_front_left, high* - Skirt immediately behind the hanging hand, upper thighs below the hem, and neck beneath the chin: Cast shadows are excessively literal and hard-edged for the painted style. The side view stamps a recognizable finger silhouette onto the skirt, while the thighs receive abrupt angular shadow cutoffs. These read as projected cutouts rather than soft contact and form shading.
  - Fix: In render.py, test SUN_ANGLE_DEG around 2–4 degrees and SHADOW_FILTER around 1.0–1.5, but pair that with a narrow transition in toon.py because the current CONSTANT ramp re-thresholds filtered shadows. Introduce per-key softness, initially around 0.02–0.04 for skin and dress, rather than changing every material. If this softens form terminators too much, separate the cast-shadow factor from the form-light ramp and soften only the former. Retain contact darkening beneath hems and the chin.
- **P1** [naturalness] *back, q34_back_right, turn_135, turn_180* - Broad central rear hair mass above the curled ends: Outside the small scratches, the back hair is almost uniformly white, so neighboring locks merge into a broad slab. Clay shows substantially more volume than the cel render communicates. The back hair shade share is only 0.19 against 0.37 in the sheet, despite the side already being over-shaded at 0.48 against 0.37; a global increase would worsen that imbalance.
  - Fix: After cleaning the slivers, reshape the rear hair’s low-frequency shading-normal field to create continuous shaded flanks on the major lock groups. Replace the single lower cylindrical volume_normal() field with smoothly blended ellipsoidal fields centered on those groups, retaining some local curl normal contribution. Keep the shared hair threshold near its current value during this step and judge front, side, and rear together; do not add a camera-dependent rear-view threshold.
- **P1** [animation] *walk q34_front_left, squat q34_front_left, forward_bend q34_front_left* - Front and side skirt panels between the waist and lower hem: Deformation produces disconnected mint-grey patches and angular shadow fragments across the skirt, most clearly in squat and walk. The material makes the cloth appear dented or mottled as it bends. The stills establish pose-dependent fragmentation; actual frame-to-frame popping has not been demonstrated.
  - Fix: Evaluate dress shading normals after deformation and smooth them over the broad cloth panels while retaining the main folds. Add a separate dress ramp-softness setting in toon.py, starting around 0.02, and compare against the hard ramp. Render short interpolations into walk and squat under a fixed light to identify which patches arise from normals and which are cast shadows. Carry any normal correction into the exported mesh and reproduce the ramp transition in the PMX toon texture.
- **P2** [naturalness] *back, q34_back_right, side* - White-to-mint transitions on the lower rear locks and outer hooked strands: Several mint ends read as solid colored caps, with abrupt steps where the tint transition meets a toon boundary. This strengthens the impression of thick molded hooks and loses the sheet’s softer transition from white hair into green tips. The existing shader already interpolates gradient colors, so the visible steps should not be assumed to come from a quantized gradient alone.
  - Fix: Inspect toon_grad independently of lighting, then lengthen compressed transitions along each affected lock to roughly the final 25–35 percent of its arc length. Smooth the attribute over adjacent longitudinal rings without blending across separate locks. In toon.py, add a small hair-specific ramp transition only where the remaining step is a lighting boundary; keep base and shade gradients synchronized.
- **P2** [naturalness] *side, back, q34_back_right* - Visible pink petticoat tiers beneath the outer skirt: The petticoat becomes a stack of broad dark pink bands in the side and rear views. Shade occupies 0.60 in both orthographic views and 0.68 in q34_back_right, making the thin ruffles read heavier and more laminated than the reference. This is a broad shadow-distribution problem, not an island-count problem.
  - Fix: Give petticoat its own entry in RAMP_BY_KEY and compare a modest threshold reduction from the current fallback of 0.30 to 0.20–0.25 with a shadows-off diagnostic. If the bands remain cast-shadow dominated, adjust their shadow-edge treatment rather than continuing to lower the threshold. Preserve a narrow darker contact region beneath each overlapping tier, and use a lighter shadow mix on the exposed pink faces.
- **P2** [naturalness] *front, q34_front_left, high* - Green upper-arm ornaments, small chest leaves, and floral hair ornaments: Small decorations alternate between nearly flat fills and tiny high-contrast shade chips. The green region contributes 14 islands in front and 16 in q34_front_left. At this scale the fragments obscure the leaf and folded-ribbon shapes instead of clarifying them.
  - Fix: Use low-frequency shading normals for each leaf blade or ribbon lobe, with the crease retained as one deliberate boundary. Add separate softness controls for leaf and green rather than applying the pale-cloth setting indiscriminately. Keep bulky ornaments casting shadows, as the pipeline already intends; suppress only confirmed thin-detail casters using levi_cast_shadow, and verify that this reduces visible fragmentation rather than merely exposing more self-shading islands.
- **P2** [fidelity] *front, side, back* - Mint hair tips and green leaf decorations: The mint tips look pale and desaturated, while several leaf faces read as bright, simple green patches instead of the sheet’s darker modeled greens. Hair interior color error remains 7.9–10.9 and green interior error 10.0–16.1, so the mismatch is not confined to outlines. Palette and shadow placement both contribute.
  - Fix: After stabilizing shadow shapes, interpolate the hair_tip tuning partway back toward SHEET_PALETTE: test a 25–50 percent move from #8CC0A2 toward #76B48E for base and from #70A386 toward #619379 for shade. Tune leaf and green independently; test slightly higher thresholds for the under-shaded leaf faces rather than darkening all ribbons and leaves together. Compare interior color error and all six views before accepting a palette change.
- **P3** [naturalness] *back, q34_back_right, high* - Internal rear-hair overlaps and curled-tip outlines: Short internal contour fragments remain visually similar to the unwanted shadow scratches, while the curl undersides carry relatively heavy lines. Even after shade-island cleanup, these contours can keep the hair looking assembled from separate pieces.
  - Fix: Use add_outline()'s existing vertex_group and min_factor controls to taper hull thickness on buried overlap edges to approximately 25–40 percent of the outer contour width. Preserve full width on the external silhouette and major intentional lock separations. Review an outline-only pass first so genuine contour fragments are not confused with shade islands.
