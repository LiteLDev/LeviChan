# Codex review: gpt-6-astra

Levi reads coherently around the turntable, but the cel shading still exposes narrow hair slivers, angular garment patches and abrupt colour boundaries that weaken the impression of continuous 3D surfaces. The principal lookdev problem is the spatial organization of shadows, not simply their total coverage: matching a region's shade percentage can still leave it visibly chipped or flat. Prioritize continuous hair normals, deformation-stable garment shading and a less fragmented petticoat shadow before further palette matching. The squat needs a geometry/weight correction alongside lookdev work, while the raised sleeves need a more convincing gravity response. These judgments use the supplied stills and targeted code inspection; temporal flicker and the exported PMX appearance remain unverified.

Scores: naturalness 6/10, animation 5/10, fidelity 7/10

## hair_back

- **P1** [naturalness] *clay back, clay q34_back_right, q34_back_right* - Lower rear curls and exposed edges of the overlapping central locks: The clay views expose angular curl sections and abrupt changes between overlapping locks; the cel ramp amplifies these into pointed shade wedges and slivers. Shader tuning cannot fully hide this geometric discontinuity without flattening the whole hair mass.
  - Fix: In hair_back.py, increase cross-section sampling selectively from 8 to 12–16 vertices on exposed curls, reduce CURL_TURN from 12 degrees toward 6–8 degrees, and halve CURL_SEG where the bend silhouette visibly kinks. Blend custom-normal contribution continuously through curl entrances and root overlaps; spend the extra faces locally rather than subdividing the entire character.

## lookdev

- **P1** [naturalness] *back, q34_back_right, islands back* - White rear hair mass, from the crown through the middle hanging locks: Numerous narrow grey cuts interrupt an otherwise nearly white hair mass, giving it a scratched or chipped appearance rather than broad, intentional cel shading. The back hair alone has 55 small shade islands, and the rear three-quarter view has 41; these are spatial defects even though their total area is small.
  - Fix: Keep the hard two-tone ramp in levi/toon.py, but correct the normal field feeding it before changing the hair threshold. Use hair_back.set_volume_normals() to blend adjacent overlapping roots toward a shared envelope normal and smoothly vary the own-normal contribution along each lock. Compare shadows-on and shadows-off renders to separate normal terminators from overlap shadows; do not try to solve this with a larger shadow filter or a global palette lift.
- **P1** [animation] *walk q34_front_left, squat q34_front_left* - Pale front skirt immediately below the waist and across the raised thigh region: The posed skirt develops detached, angular mint patches across what should read as broad continuous cloth. The squat is particularly conspicuous: the shading makes the deformed surface look dented and triangulated. The stills establish fragmentation, although they do not establish flicker between frames.
  - Fix: Inspect the evaluated skirt normals after armature deformation and establish a consistent triangulation before skinning/export so nonplanar quads do not change their effective diagonal. Smooth normals across intended continuous folds while preserving actual hem edges. In toon.py, make transition softness configurable per palette key and test a small 0.015–0.025 half-width on dress materials only after correcting the deformed surface; retain hard endpoints and verify the same treatment in the PMX toon texture.
- **P2** [naturalness] *q34_back_right, side, high* - Pink petticoat under the rear and side skirt hem: The pink tiers break into alternating broad dark strips and pale flaps, making the underside read as stacked cut sheets. Rear three-quarter shade coverage reaches 0.64, but the more important defect is that the shadow follows many individual folds instead of describing a coherent recessed volume.
  - Fix: Give petticoat its own RAMP_BY_KEY entry rather than inheriting the global 0.30 threshold, and evaluate it separately from the dress. In render.py's petticoat receiver group, test replacing fine tier self-shadowing with a smooth tier envelope while retaining the existing skirt-hem contact caster. Tune the proxy lift in 0.5 cm increments from its current 2 cm, checking side and rear three-quarter views so the correction does not erase the contact under the outer skirt.
- **P2** [naturalness] *back, q34_back_right, clay back* - Broad central back hair surface above the curled tips: The central hair reads as a mostly flat white curtain, while the clay image reveals considerably more layered volume. Small dark seams carry too much of the form description because there is too little coherent broad shading between the principal masses.
  - Fix: After removing the narrow islands, tune the envelope-versus-own-normal blend to produce one or two continuous shade regions along the major locks. Sweep the hair threshold from 0.42 toward 0.46–0.48 only as a secondary adjustment, reviewing all six angles together; the side is already more shaded than the reference, so a global threshold increase alone is unlikely to resolve this.
- **P2** [naturalness] *back, q34_back_right* - White-to-mint transition on the lower rear locks and hook-shaped tips: Several tips read as solid mint attachments beneath white locks, with angular transitions that compound the segmented appearance of the hair. This is a colour-field and surface-sampling issue as well as a lighting issue.
  - Fix: Keep base and shade gradients separate from the lighting threshold, as _build_toon_tree() already does. Widen the spatial transition in hair_back.grad_global() to approximately 8–12 cm where the current fade compresses onto a bend, and insert enough longitudinal samples to provide at least four intervals through that fade. Preserve gradient-aware outline colours and check that the exported gradient texture reproduces the same smooth transition.
- **P2** [fidelity] *front, side, q34_front_left* - Leaf ornaments at the head and ankles, and green sleeve attachment ornaments: The green pieces often read as uniformly bright cutouts, while the sheet uses stronger internal dark-green separation. Front and side green shade coverage is substantially lower than the reference, and the reported interior colour error remains high, so outline alignment alone does not explain the mismatch.
  - Fix: Tune leaf and green independently in RAMP_BY_KEY instead of moving both together: test leaf thresholds of 0.55 and 0.60 while retaining the current green threshold for thin cords. Judge the larger leaves and bows separately from ribbons, then adjust their base/shade colours in spec.PALETTE_TUNING. Preserve broad leaf shading rather than accepting additional tiny dark patches as improved coverage.
- **P3** [fidelity] *front, q34_front_left* - Long green waist ribbon tails over the pale skirt: The ribbon tails read as relatively heavy, uniformly dark strokes next to the lighter garment contours. At three-quarter angles their fill and hull outline merge, reducing the sense that they are thin three-dimensional ribbons.
  - Fix: Use add_outline()'s width or vertex-group control to reduce ribbon-tail hull width to roughly 50–65% of the default while retaining the bow's stronger silhouette. Preserve a visible green fill strip through bends and inspect the result at the supplied render scale before changing the shared green line colour.

## rig

- **P1** [animation] *squat front, squat q34_front_left* - Front skirt panels and petticoat tiers over the bent thighs: The squat lifts the front garment into a stiff shelf and crowds its layers rather than producing a continuous drape over the thighs. This deformation also creates the disconnected cel-shadow patches, so lookdev and cloth weighting need to be corrected together.
  - Fix: In levi/rig.py, distribute the skirt's collision-like rotation over the lower chain segments instead of concentrating SKIRT_PUSH_JOINTS at joint 1, preserving the total added rotation rather than applying the full gain twice. Rebalance _skirt_weights() across height so the waist remains stable and the outer skirt and pink tiers follow corresponding chains. Maintain approximately 0.5–1 cm separation between corresponding layers in the evaluated squat, and verify the PMX physics equivalent because the Blender push driver is not exported directly.
- **P1** [animation] *arms_up front, arms_up q34_front_left* - Both sleeve bags beside and above the head: The sleeve volumes largely rotate upward with the arms, producing upright padded masses and sharp transverse shade breaks. Their elevated shape is visibly inconsistent with loose hanging fabric, even though the wrists remain attached.
  - Fix: Keep the sleeve attachment bands following the upper arms, but increase the gravity counter-rotation on the free drape chains from the documented 40% toward 60–70% as an initial test. Spread that correction down the drape segments and smoothly increase their influence away from the arm instead of creating a rigid upper/lower split. Check head clearance and cuff continuity in both raised-arm views, then reproduce the fall with PMX sleeve-body constraints and physics.
