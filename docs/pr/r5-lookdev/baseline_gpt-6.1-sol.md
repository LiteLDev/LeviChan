# Codex review: gpt-6.1-sol

The character is readable and the overall palette is close, but the lookdev still exposes procedural surface structure through broken shadow shapes, thin hair slivers, and angular patches on pale cloth and skin. The highest priority is to separate cast-shadow artifacts from normal-driven terminators, then clean each source while preserving a deliberate two-tone finish. Posed views reveal substantially harsher shadow fragmentation than the rest views, so material tuning needs to include squat, walk, and forward bend. The back hair also needs a more coherent large-scale shade design: its nearly white central mass contrasts with heavily shaded curled tips and does not describe the volume as clearly as the reference. Resolve shadow topology and shading-normal continuity before adjusting regional shade percentages or final colours.

Scores: naturalness 6/10, animation 5/10, fidelity 7/10

The diagnostic marks 55 hair islands in back and 41 in the rear three-quarter view; their vertical distribution makes the surface read as scratched or chipped rather than as a coherent shaded hair mass.
