The most important lookdev fixes are to stabilize hair shading normals and remove the disconnected skirt shade patches introduced by posing; increasing shadow-map filtering will not address those underlying problems. The back-hair tips also combine a mint transition with an abrupt change in shading-normal behavior, making several curls appear capped with a different material. After those issues are resolved, retune the broad shadow coverage on the rear hair, dress, petticoat and green ornaments without chasing aggregate shade percentages at the expense of coherent forms. The supplied poses show no obvious catastrophic tearing, but the squat exposes a clear cloth-shading failure, and the Blender-specific caster policy needs an equivalent export strategy before the PMX appearance can be considered dependable.

**gpt-6-astra:** Levi reads coherently around the turntable, but the cel shading still exposes narrow hair slivers, angular garment patches and abrupt colour boundaries that weaken the impression of continuous 3D surfaces. The principal lookdev problem is the spatial organization of shadows, not simply their total coverage: matching a region's shade percentage can still leave it visibly chipped or flat. Prioritize continuous hair normals, deformation-stable garment shading and a less fragmented petticoat shadow before further palette matching. The squat needs a geometry/weight correction alongside lookdev work, while the raised sleeves need a more convincing gravity response. These judgments use the supplied stills and targeted code inspection; temporal flicker and the exported PMX appearance remain unverified.
