# Codex review: gpt-6.1-sol

The back hair preserves the broad reference silhouette, but its lower half still reads as overlapping sculpted strips rather than a coherent volume of hair when viewed obliquely. The most urgent problems are the intersecting lower locks, the exposed flat backing curtain, and the broad outer crescents whose faces remain too planar through their turns. Repair those shapes before adjusting the toon ramp: custom volume normals currently conceal some geometric problems while leaving other strand boundaries as small chips and abrupt wedges. In the pose tests, the forward bend still carries the outer hair backward like a cape; the head turn and swing also reveal smaller transition problems. Scores below describe the hair_back review scope, and the supplied static poses do not establish PMX physics stability.

Scores: naturalness 5/10, animation 6/10, fidelity 6/10
