# Codex review: gpt-6-astra

The back hair preserves the character's broad silhouette, but its three-dimensional construction still reads as overlapping sculpted strips: the lower curls crowd into repeated hooks, the outer locks resemble rigid fins, and the pale central mass loses convincing strand flow. Clay views expose abrupt overlaps and angular transitions that the cel shading partly conceals, while the cel views retain isolated chips and slivers around the same lower-lock junctions. In squat and forward_bend, the hanging lengths visibly carry the torso's pitch instead of settling into a curved drape; these are major follow/deformation issues, although the supplied stills do not establish how the exported PMX physics behaves. First revise the lower-lock paths and overlap hierarchy, then give the hanging lengths a gravity-aware bend with collision clearance, and finally retune normals and strand separation. Scores and pose ratings are scoped to hair_back and its visible interactions; matching the reference's finer wave pattern should follow those structural fixes.

Scores: naturalness 5/10, animation 5/10, fidelity 6/10

## hair_back

- **P1** [naturalness] *clay back, clay q34_back_right, q34_back_right* - Lower central hair mass, where the descending primary locks meet the upward-turning curls above the skirt: Several lower locks converge into crowded, nested hooks with abrupt pointed overlaps and dark wedge-shaped crevices. Their paths become difficult to follow through depth, making the ends read as interlocking sculpted pieces rather than a few coherent locks; apparent surface crossings warrant checking in the mesh.
  - Fix: In levi/parts/hair_back.py, revise the curled MAIN definitions together with SWEEP rather than changing each silhouette independently. Stagger curl starts vertically by roughly 1–2 cm and separate neighboring curl planes by 0.8–1.5 cm as initial values; reduce widths where the descending locks enter the turns. Preserve a consistent front-to-back order along each overlap and check full swept sections for collisions, since _clear_rings() currently addresses skirt clearance rather than lock-to-lock clearance.
- **P1** [naturalness] *q34_front_left, q34_back_right, clay q34_front_left, turn_045* - Both outer crescent locks extending beyond the sleeves: The long outer locks present broad, nearly uninterrupted faces ending in narrow upturned hooks. At oblique angles they read as rigid fins attached behind the sleeves, with insufficient change in depth and section orientation to communicate a flowing lock of hair.
  - Fix: Reshape the last OUTER_LEFT definition and its mirror: introduce a gradual 1–2 cm depth variation through the lower descending run, distribute the turn over a longer section, and reduce width before the tightest bend. Replace the near-constant terminal tw values with a smooth roll through the hook, maintaining a rounded lens section instead of thinning the silhouette into a blade. Judge the result at 45-degree increments before refitting the front silhouette.
- **P1** [animation] *squat q34_front_left, forward_bend q34_front_left* - Hanging lengths from the shoulder blades through the outer crescents and lower tips: The long lengths are carried backward by torso pitch with little visible settling, producing a stiff, projecting silhouette in both poses. The rig documentation explicitly retains forward-bend inheritance while applying gravity compensation only to sideways bending, which is consistent with this visible behavior.
  - Fix: Keep the scalp attachment, but add forward-pitch compensation progressively along the hanging chains in levi/rig.py rather than counter-rotating the entire hair root. Start with 30–50% compensation distributed over the lower three segments, then constrain the resulting drape against back, sleeve, and skirt collision surfaces with approximately 0.8–1.2 cm clearance. Apply a compatible settled setup to the PMX hair bodies and verify these same poses after physics settles; do not assume the Blender follow behavior proves the PMX result.
- **P2** [naturalness] *clay back, clay q34_back_right, clay side* - Lower third of the main locks, especially transitions from broad descending sections into curls: Some section transitions look angular or pinched despite the otherwise smooth upper mass. The local silhouette and creases suggest changes in width, frame orientation, or clearance displacement that are too abrupt; adding uniform subdivision alone would preserve those underlying kinks.
  - Fix: In clump(), extend adaptive sampling to account for cross-section width, twist, and the final displacement from _clear_rings(), not only centerline distance and turn. Use parallel transport for the section frame, then apply authored twist. Begin with 16–20 vertices around exposed curls and 3–4 px longitudinal spacing at their transitions, and smooth the clearance displacement over neighboring rings before regenerating normals.
- **P2** [naturalness] *back, q34_back_right, clay back* - Broad white central curtain from the nape to the lower lobes: The central mass reads as a largely undifferentiated sheet above a cluster of busy tips. Broad sections merge visually while a few narrow grooves appear abruptly, so the major locks do not maintain legible root-to-tip flow.
  - Fix: Revise MAIN and WAVES to establish three or four continuous primary ridges, with shallow valleys that develop gradually below the nape. Use roughly 0.5–1 cm of controlled depth separation as a starting point and tuck secondary endpoints beneath those primaries. Keep the backing curtain inside the lock envelope so exposed shell areas do not bridge away the intended valleys.
- **P2** [naturalness] *back, q34_back_right, side, islands back, islands q34_back_right* - Central vertical strand junctions and lower curl roots: Small disconnected grey-green marks and narrow terminator slivers interrupt the pale hair. The red overlay identifies several on the back mass and curl junctions; the supplied hair-region totals also include hair_front, so they should not all be attributed to this part.
  - Fix: After resolving the overlaps, adjust set_volume_normals() so the transition from the shared volume field to free-lock normals varies smoothly along each lock and around its section. Replace the facing-dependent edge suppression with an explicitly authored section mask where it creates abrupt normal changes. Test hair_back_side with its cast shadows disabled to distinguish cast-shadow slivers from normal-field islands, then correct the responsible overlap or normal transition rather than globally lowering the hair ramp threshold.
- **P2** [fidelity] *sheet back versus render back, sheet side versus render side* - Lower lobes and side-facing curl group: The reference has long, overlapping S-shaped flows leading into varied open curls. The model concentrates much of that structure into a shorter-looking row of similar U-shaped ends, while the side-facing curl openings are less clearly articulated.
  - Fix: Vary the individual MAIN curl radii and start heights instead of using a repeated terminal motif, and extend selected descending paths before they turn. Reposition SWEEP so one dominant side-facing opening remains readable between the surrounding locks. Fit the major negative spaces in side and back views while retaining the depth separation established in the structural pass.
- **P2** [fidelity] *sheet back versus render back, q34_back_right* - White-to-mint transition across the lower main locks and curl tips: The reference uses differentiated green undersides and tips to explain overlapping locks, while the render's lower group tends toward a similar pale mint across multiple adjacent curls. This weakens their separation and makes the tips read as one cluster.
  - Fix: Retain grad_global() as the shared baseline, but add restrained per-primary gradient offsets and section-based underside variation to the hair_tip attribute. Start with onset offsets of 10–20 reference pixels between neighboring primaries, blending continuously along the spine and keeping the upward-turning tips monotonic. Check the result separately from lighting so stronger tip color does not merely substitute for missing curl volume.
- **P3** [fidelity] *back, q34_back_right* - Interior strand boundaries through the middle and lower back hair: Interior lines are selectively absent through broad areas and then reappear as short accents near the tips, whereas the reference uses longer, intentional curves to describe the major strand paths.
  - Fix: After the geometry and shading pass, replace purely step-threshold-driven interior outline selection with a small set of authored boundary strips or stable boundary weights following the primary locks. Keep the inverted hull for the outside silhouette, and taper the selected interior lines over several rings rather than using isolated on/off vertex decisions.
