# Codex review: gpt-6-astra

The hair is recognizable and broadly matches the reference silhouette, but the rear mass still reads as overlapping molded strips rather than a coherent arrangement of soft, rounded locks. The most important fixes are the exposed, angular transitions above the back curls, the flattened curl sections at oblique angles, and the crowded hair-to-arm contact in the arms-up pose. Custom volume normals hide much of the strand structure across the upper back while leaving isolated slivers farther down; correct the underlying overlap and section shapes before tuning those normals further. The front hair is stronger overall, although its long face-framing locks and fringe tips remain heavy and rigid compared with the sheet. Scores and pose ratings below concern the focused hair parts and their interactions; still images cannot establish temporal stability or PMX physics behavior.

Scores: naturalness 5/10, animation 6/10, fidelity 6/10
