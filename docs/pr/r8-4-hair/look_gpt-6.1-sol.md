# Codex review: gpt-6.1-sol

The hair preserves the character's overall identity, but the rear three-quarter and clay views expose thin overlapping surfaces, abrupt lower-lock terminations, and curls that read as cutout loops rather than flowing bundles. The most important modeling change is to rebuild the lower back-hair transitions as continuous rounded volumes, with the backing curtain fully concealed and neighboring locks separated deliberately through depth. Raised arms and the forward bend expose angular interior hair surfaces that need coordinated shell and lock deformation; the other supplied poses show no comparable collapse. After those fixes, refine the front hair's cheek flicks and shoulder framing, then clean the local normal transitions responsible for chipped shade patches without flattening the entire hairstyle.
