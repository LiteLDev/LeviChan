**gpt-6.1-sol:** The skirt has a coherent bell volume, but its lower folds and hem still read as a fitted shell in the side and three-quarter views: several edges flatten into blades, and the broad panels do not consistently roll into the scallops. Animation exposes the larger weakness: the squat compresses the skirt into a shelf, while the walking pose produces abrupt angular shade patches across otherwise smooth cloth. Prioritize the hem's radial return, deformation through the middle and lower rings, and coordinated clearance between the skirt and petticoat before refining the reference silhouette. The existing 153 azimuth samples and 13 longitudinal rings provide a useful starting point; the next pass should improve their distribution, shape and weights rather than uniformly increasing mesh density.

**gpt-6-astra:** The skirt maintains a strong overall bell silhouette, but its broad, shallow panels and sharply scalloped hem read more like a shaped shell than soft folded cloth in the three-quarter and high views. The most urgent problem is deformation: walking and squatting introduce isolated angular shade patches across the skirt, while the squat lifts the lower garment into a broad shelf with little convincing downward drape. Improve the skirt's longitudinal sampling and chain deformation together, then refine the depth and curvature of individual folds before adjusting shading. Reference matching should concentrate on the rolled hem transitions and distribution of folds, rather than increasing silhouette IoU at the expense of three-dimensional form. These scores assess the skirt and its immediate garment interactions; static pose images cannot establish temporal stability or PMX physics performance.
